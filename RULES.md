# Barroom Brawl Rules

Status: Working draft 0.1

## Objective

Checkmate the opposing king.

## Board

Barroom Brawl is played on a 5×5 board with files `a` through `e` and ranks `1` through `5`.

## Starting armies

Light begins with a king and queen on its first rank and five pawns filling its second rank.

Dark begins with a king, rook, two bishops, and knight filling its first rank. Dark has no pawns.

The exact back-rank arrangements have not yet been selected.

## Movement

Unless stated otherwise, pieces move, capture, give check, and deliver checkmate as they do in orthodox chess.

## Proposed simplifications

- No castling.
- No en passant.
- Pawns do not have an initial two-square move.
- A pawn promotes upon reaching the opposite back rank.

## Decisions still required

- Exact starting squares for both armies.
- Which side moves first.
- Whether promotion is automatically to a queen or offers a choice.
- Whether stalemate is a draw or a win for either side.
- Repetition and move-count draw rules.
- Whether any orthodox insufficient-material rules apply.

These decisions should be treated as testable balance parameters rather than finalized by convention.
